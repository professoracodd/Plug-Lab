# Plug Lab

A choose-your-path assignment for plant propagation. Teams research, cost, price and plan a plug tray crop, then compare their businesses with the class.

## Files
- `index.html`: the student app. It opens on the **Lab** tab, which walks teams through 13 steps.
- `board.html`: the class board. Load every team's results file to compare them side by side.
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, icons: these let students install the app and use it offline.

## How a step works
1. The step's **Go fill it in** button glows. Tapping it opens the right tab, and every field still needed pulses white.
2. When the fields on a tab are done, a glowing **Next** button takes students to the next tab. When the whole step is done, **Submit** takes them back to the Lab tab. Nothing moves on its own.
3. Back on the Lab tab, they check their numbers, write their **Sources and notes**, and tap **Submit step**. The notes are printed on the fact sheet.

## Running the assignment
1. Turn on GitHub Pages for this repo and share the link to `index.html` with students.
2. Each team picks a path: A mother stock, B buy in, C collect offsite, or D seed.
3. In step 12, give each team a contract order: how many plug trays, and a ship date.
4. In step 13, teams export a one-page **fact sheet**, as a PDF or an image, for their presentation. They also save a **results file** and turn it in.
5. Open `board.html`, tap **Add team files**, and select every team's file. The files stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Each team's work is saved on the device they use. Teams should stick to one phone or laptop. To move a team's work to another device, open the results file with **Menu › Restore a backup**.
